Why it works this way

Dr. Marion Blank's method, faithfully

One word at a time

Each session teaches a single word through ~30 varied trials — see it, spot it, build it, use it, read it — instead of skimming many words shallowly.

Whole words, never sounded out

Most English words can't be decoded by rules. Words are always spoken and learned whole — letter tiles are silent; the finished word speaks.

Errors are never practiced

A wrong tap is redirected instantly and every trial ends with Emma making the correct response herself. No red marks, no X's — and no wrong answer ever gets rehearsed.
